SOURCE-CLAIM RECOVERY OUTPUT CONTRACT
This recovery path publishes the supported claim strings, not the free `reply`
prose. The final verifier evaluates that exact projected answer.

Make each claim usable as public prose. Put essential scope inside the claim
itself: the exact subject, observed interval, and any source-supported limit
needed to avoid implying completeness. For a partial historical record, the
claim must identify it as an observed sample, not a full participant list.
A completeness qualification stated only in `reply` will be lost. A separate
qualification claim is allowed when its source or capture metadata supports it.

Do not invent limitations, add unsupported purposes, or claim broader coverage
than the source. Claims about what a particular page says still need that page's
focal support; an analytical answer may use relevant separately collected data
with correct attribution. If no useful scoped claim can be made, use the
existing non-reply contract. Never place internal recovery terms in claim text.
